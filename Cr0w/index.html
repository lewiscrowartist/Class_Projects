Luis Ulloa

I am an illustrator, painter, and designer whose work blends the bold energy of street art with the imaginative worlds of comics, manga, and video games. Born in Southern California to Mexican immigrant parents, I grew up navigating multiple cultural identities an experience that continues to shape the vibrant characters and graphic narratives in my art.

My creative journey began in childhood, when my restlessness in school led me to sketch characters from Nintendo Power magazines. The futuristic heroism of Mega Man sparked a lifelong fascination with robots, technology, and the visual language of futurism. In high school, I discovered graffiti and street art, a turning point that gave me not only a visual style but also a name: Crow. The crow’s dark, striking presence carried the edge I admired, even while my favorite bird the duck remained a softer, humorous inspiration rooted in my love for Donald Duck comics.

Since relocating to Chattanooga, Tennessee in 2017, I have continued pursuing my creative path, now inspired by the life I share with my wife and son. Whether through illustration, painting, or design, I strive to merge storytelling and style, crafting visuals that feel familiar, expressive, and distinctly my own.
